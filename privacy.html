Privacy Policy

How Go Hire Consultancy collects, uses, and protects your information

Last updated: September 2026

1. Who We Are

Go Hire Consultancy is a manpower placement agency that connects skilled workers with employers in India, the Gulf, and abroad. Our website is gohireconsultancy.com. When you apply for work through this website or contact us, you share personal information with us. This policy explains what we collect, how we use it, and your rights over it.

2. Information We Collect

When you submit an application, we collect the following:

  • Identity data — full name, gender
  • Contact data — email address, phone number (with country code)
  • Professional data — trade or skill, years of experience, preferred work location
  • Documents — resume or CV that you upload during the application
  • Payment data — payment status and order reference (we do not store card or bank details — see Section 4)
  • Communications — messages exchanged with our team through the website chat

If you register as an employer, we collect your company name, contact person, email, phone number, and the details of the roles you wish to fill.
